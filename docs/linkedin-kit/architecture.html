Covenant Agent · how it works

The model reads the agreement once.
Code checks everything. A person signs off.

Code: deterministic, tested Model: fills fixed structures Person: decides and signs off
1Public filings from SEC EDGARInput

The credit agreement and its amendments (about 250 to 330 pages), plus the 10-Q and 10-K for reported ratios.

2Read the documentCode

Live text with struck redline words removed. Every section, definition and cross-reference, packed into the covenant sections and each definition they depend on.

3Extract into fixed structuresClaude

Covenant levels by test date, relief periods and step-ups, and every add-back and deduction in EBITDA, debt and interest, each with its cap and exact quote.

4Check everythingCode
Every quote is verbatim in the agreementEvery number appears in its quote Every clause coveredDates and overlapsClaude vs GPT compared Each quote found on its page

What code can't confirm becomes a review card, ranked high or low priority.

5Review and sign offAnalyst

An MCP App inside ChatGPT or Claude. Each card sits next to the clause, highlighted on the agreement page. Confirm it, change the value, or note the document that's needed. Then sign off: 6 to 10 cards per deal, not 300 pages.

6Covenant testing workbookCode, no model

One click builds the Excel the analyst tests with: a calendar of every test date and level to maturity, plain-English definitions, and a test sheet where the agreement's caps apply by formula. Every quarter after that runs without a model.
